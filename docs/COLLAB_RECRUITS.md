# Optional local recruits and elite AI (0.3.1)

The server owner's 2026-10-21 request adds 丰川祥子 and 结城理 to tier-5 and tier-6 owned-operator selection without new
bonds. It also installs AI·阿尔图罗 v0.2.0 and AI·拉维妮娅 v0.1.2. The original five UI/room customizations remain.

## Recruits

The local `collab-recruits` content plugin adds two namespaced `backups.units` records and their DIY selection metadata.
It adds no shop chess, bonds, factions, bands, items or garrisons. Existing generated `data/*.json` stays unchanged.
The reviewed native builders in `OPTIONAL_OPERATOR_KITS` are available through explicit `kitRef`; they are excluded from
`KITTED_CHARS` and `OPERATOR_KITS`, so their presence alone cannot expand the vanilla owned pool or golden scenarios.

The records were generated by the host's `localOfficialUnits` builder from the six official tables, full potential,
E2 Lv1/rank4 for normal forms and E2 Lv60/rank7 for elite forms, module stage 1 at tier 5 and stage 3 at tier 6.
Table fingerprint: `sha256:c9cec47e05582306d8386ed3d58353e902862c2490e794f010e6814bb3b1ea21`.
Official main/sub-power membership gives 丰川祥子 the existing fallback `emptyShip` and 结城理 the existing
`lateranoShip` (hidden Laterano membership). No Ave Mujica/SEES covenant is introduced.

One source BB key, 丰川 S2 `attack@attack_speed`, is normalized to the existing `attack_speed` key and read under that
name by its builder. Its exact official values (80/110) are retained; the plugin's bounds checks are not relaxed.
`recruit-visuals` is a separately packed cosmetic companion. Its models are parsed into the lock file, not supplied
with handwritten clip metadata. The reviewed Makoto persona roles are wired to his specific spine ids in the renderer.

Kit source: https://github.com/remember-4/Stronghold-Protocol-test at
`5bf3f0918864322d418f0bf74ddcfae0c3113f7a`, `ops/op-oblvns.js` and `ops/op-makoto.js`, GPL-3.0-or-later.
Art source: Forge Mods historical Ave Mujica 1.1.0 (`0dc1a4fc`) and Makoto Yuki 1.0.1 (`93ae0ffc`), kept outside Git.
These are community adaptations of the official skill descriptions, not decoded client implementations.
[ASSUMED] The retained source uses straight homing notes, omits piano piercing/random arcs, has no Fever music/UI gauge,
and assumes Makoto is the only SEES member. Its S3 persona changes on a lethal hit, without an additional manual recast.
Do not describe this as complete client fidelity. All three skills and the normal/elite/module combinations are exercised.

To use the two operators, the host selects `collab-recruits` and its optional art companion in gameplay plugins before
starting a room, then selects either operator in a tier-5 or tier-6 DIY slot. Closing those plugin selections restores
the original pool. The room is correctly marked as modified. Neither operator is injected into ordinary shop stock.

## AI source extensions

Source: https://github.com/HankDUMPLINGZhong/Stronghold-Protocol-AI at
`7d4e051b990a0b63578e33bddac44fe2c068b5e3`. The released addon policy bundles and hashes are unchanged. Runtime copies add only loop-cap and internal-label
comments required by the host; a byte-comparison test strips precisely those comments to verify the original policy.
The waiting-room portraits use the existing pure URL helper to retain the vanilla lazy asset-loading boundary. Each copied addon
includes its GPL license and installation tools. Install Arturia first, then Lavinia; uninstall in reverse order.
Private `.arturia-addon` / `.lavinia-addon` recovery journals stay local and are never committed.

The waiting-room menu adds the selected elite AI. Ordinary AI remains unchanged. Both require a supported difficulty;
the existing source refuses modified-content rooms. That restriction is retained: choose a vanilla room for elite AI,
or a collab-recruits room for the new operators with ordinary AI. Do not silently remove the guard to claim compatibility.

The backend must restart after installation. Public HTTP, WS persona identity, fixed band picks, actual battle rounds,
prep timer, speed voting and renderer recovery are checked on an isolated candidate before switching the live service.

Validation: native kit layout and vanilla DIY boundaries; real DIY skill/form/module battles; immutable original bonds
and chess; AI package tests and upstream installer/coexistence tests; existing targeted tests, tooling and golden results.
